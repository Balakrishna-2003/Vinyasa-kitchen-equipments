Who We Are

Built in Mangalore, Trusted Across India

Vinyasa Kitchen Equipments began with a simple frustration: hotel and restaurant owners were choosing between kitchens that looked good and kitchens that actually held up under daily commercial use. We set out to make both non-negotiable.

Today, we design, fabricate and install complete stainless steel kitchen systems in-house — from the first layout sketch to the last service call — for hotels, restaurants, cloud kitchens, hospitals and institutions across Karnataka and beyond.

  • In-house design, fabrication & installation team
  • Food-grade stainless steel on every build
  • One point of contact from quote to after-sales

Our Vision

To be the most trusted name in commercial kitchen manufacturing in India — known as much for our after-sales support as for the quality of our stainless steel.

Our Mission

To equip every commercial kitchen we touch with equipment that performs reliably under pressure — so our clients can focus on their food and their guests, not their equipment.
